Tourist Places

Our city boasts a rich history and offers several tourist attractions that blend heritage with modernity.

  1. Heritage Fort — A 16th-century fort with stunning architecture
  2. City Museum — Showcasing 500 years of city history
  3. Botanical Garden — Home to 1000+ plant species
  4. Tech Park — Interactive science and technology center
  5. Lakeside Promenade — Evening walks with city views

How to Reach

By Air

Smart City International Airport is located 15 km from the city center with regular flights to all major cities.

By Train

Smart City Junction is well connected to major cities. Express and Superfast trains are available daily.

By Road

National Highway NH-44 passes through the city. State-run and private buses operate from the central bus stand.
